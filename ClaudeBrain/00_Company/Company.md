# Company

> AddonManager is a personal project with potential for open-source release to the Linux WoW community.

## Contents

- [[Vision]] — What AddonManager aspires to be
- [[Values]] — Principles guiding development decisions

## Overview

This is a solo project built by and for a Linux WoW player. The immediate goal is a working personal tool; the longer-term possibility is opening it up for other Linux WoW players who lack a native addon management solution.

The "company" here is effectively one person — decisions are fast, scope is controlled, and quality over quantity is the guiding principle.
