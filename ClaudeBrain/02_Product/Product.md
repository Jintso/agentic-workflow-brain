## Overview

AddonManager starts as a personal tool for one Linux WoW player. Milestone 1 is intentionally narrow: download an addon from a GitHub link for the correct WoW flavor, and check for updates.

Future scope may expand based on personal use and potential open-source adoption. See [[Vision]] for long-term direction.
